The SVP Team would like to welcome everyone back from the summer holidays and wish everyone well as they return to their normal routines!

September can be busy and, looking at the SVP agenda, it is certainly shaping up that way. We kick off with our Bereavement Support Group drop-in session on Wednesday after the 10am Mass. The following weekend, our 'Gifted' shop will be open after the Masses. Later in the month, our annual SVP Mass of Thanksgiving will be held on Friday, Sept. 25, at 7:30pm. Everyone is welcome, so please bring your family and friends if you can. 

Alongside these events, we will continue our regular visits to people in their homes, care homes and the refugee detention centre, as well as running our weekly food bank. If you would like to help with any of these initiatives, please don't hesitate to speak with us. 

Finally, as always, please remain aware of people in need in our community and [contact us](../../pages-output/contact/) if you think the SVP can help. We'll always do our best.

God bless.
