Our annual SVP Mass will be celebrated on Friday, 25 September, at 7:30 pm. Light refreshments will be provided in the hall afterwards. This is a Mass of thanksgiving for all the support we receive from our parishioners throughout the year, so everyone is invited to attend. Our parish is also blessed with B-Attitudes, our team for young people, and Mini-Vinnies, our team for primary school pupils, so we are hoping for a really strong turnout. Please put the date in your diary and invite family, friends and neighbours. We are always looking for new members, and you never know who may feel called to join us.

Finally, as always, please remain aware of anyone in need in our community and [contact us](/pages-output/contact/) if you think the SVP can help. We'll always do our best.

God bless.
