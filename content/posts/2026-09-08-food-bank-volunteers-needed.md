Firstly, a reminder of our annual SVP Mass on Friday, 25 September, at 7:30pm. Light refreshments will be provided in the hall afterwards.

Secondly, as our food bank heads into its 11th autumn and winter season, we are appealing for additional adults (aged 18 or over) to volunteer on Saturdays. The operational hours are 11am to 2pm, but you don't need to commit to every Saturday. Please consider responding to this appeal by contacting us. You will find that the Holy Spirit will reward you many times over, and you'll make a whole new bunch of friends. We really look forward to hearing from you!

Finally, as always, please remain aware of the needs in our community, and [contact us](/pages-output/contact/) if you think the SVP can help. We'll always do our best.

God bless.
