Just a reminder that our annual SVP Mass will be celebrated next Friday, 25 September, at 7:30pm. Light refreshments will be provided in the hall afterwards.

This is a Mass of thanksgiving for all the support we receive from you, our parishioners, throughout the year, so you are all invited to attend. Our parish is also blessed with a young people's SVP group, the B-Attitudes, and a team of primary school pupils, the Mini-Vinnies, so we are hoping for a really strong turnout. Please remember to invite family, friends and neighbours as well! We are always on the lookout for new members, so if you feel inspired by the evening, why not have a chat with one of the team and let the Holy Spirit guide you from there?

Finally, as always, please remain aware of those in need in our community and [contact us](/pages-output/contact/) if you think the SVP can help. We'll always do our best.

God bless.
