When you are working at the coalface, it can be easy to lose sight of the bigger picture. At a recent monthly meeting, your SVP team asked themselves some questions: Are we doing enough for our local parish and community? How do we give back to our parishioners, whose support underpins everything we do? Are there gaps in what we do?

We, the SVP members, would love to hear your thoughts and ideas about these questions. Please have a think and share your feedback with any of us. Needless to say, everything you share will be kept strictly confidential. And if you come up with ideas that you would like to help put into action, why not join the team? With the nights drawing in, you might like something different to do this winter!

Finally, as always, please remain aware of need in our community and [contact us](/pages-output/contact/) if you think the SVP can help. We'll always do our best.

God bless.
