Thank you to everyone who dropped by our fundraising shop, Gifted, last weekend. Your support for our initiatives is what keeps us energised! Rest assured that we are now busy sourcing the items that you ordered. 

Remember, too, that we are still welcoming new food bank volunteers, including opportunities for participants in the Duke of Edinburgh's Award. Autumn, and particularly winter, are always challenging seasons for those who need the food bank, so your help will be especially valuable during these months. [Get in touch](/pages-output/contact/) if you can help on Saturdays from 11am to 2pm. 

Finally, as always, please keep an eye out for anyone in need in our community and contact us if you think the SVP can be of help. We'll always do our best. 

God bless.
