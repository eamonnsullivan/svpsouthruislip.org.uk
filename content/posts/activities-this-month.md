As we enter a new month, here's a reminder of what's coming up at the SVP.

Our bereavement support group meets in the hall after the 10 am Mass
on Wednesday 2 December. Everyone is welcome to join us for a chat over tea or
coffee and biscuits, whether or not they've been bereaved. Nine people
came along to November's session, and it was lovely to hear the
conversation flow.

Next weekend, 5-6 December, we will hold our final SVP Gifted shop of
2026. We'll have plenty of Advent and Christmas items, so drop by and
say hello. Something might catch your eye!

Please pick up a [reverse advent calendar](/foodbank/#advent) from the
porch if you're planning to donate to the food bank this Christmas.
You can bring your completed boxes directly to the food bank on
Saturdays. The best time to drop them off is between 11 am and 11:30
am, before we open to the public at noon.

Finally, as always, watch out for each other and [contact
us](/contact/) if you think the SVP can be of help. We'll always do
our best.

God bless.
