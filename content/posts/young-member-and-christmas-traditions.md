Our parish is really blessed with an SVP presence for people of all ages. At St. Swithun Wells' primary school we have the Mini-Vinnies. Our parish B-Attitudes group is for secondary school pupils. Finally, we have the adult SVP team for anyone over 18 years of age. Guided by the Holy Spirit, these three groups aim to make our community a more loving and caring place in which to live. 

As an example of this, the B-Attitudes are holding a cake sale this weekend after all Masses. The money raised will help buy Christmas gifts for the residents of our local care home, Brackenbridge House. The B-Attitudes have been visiting Brackenbridge House since 2017, accompanied by adult SVP members. Taking Christmas presents there has become a tradition. The visits bring joy to the residents and are a lovely experience for the young people too. Please support the cake sale as best you can. 

As always, please be aware of those in need in our community and [contact us](/contact/) if you think the SVP can be of help. We'll always do our best. 

God bless.
